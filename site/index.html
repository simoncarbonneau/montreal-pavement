Montréal Arterial Pavement Condition — 2024 Snapshot

This map shows the 2024 pavement condition of Montréal's arterial road segments. For A3, the pavement dataset is joined to Montréal's truck-regulation network using shared road-segment IDs (ID_TRC).

This map is for

Montréal borough infrastructure planners and road-maintenance teams.

What you can do here

Filter pavement segments by truck-regulation category. Roads stay coloured by PCI condition, and the map updates the segment count, average PCI and percentage in poor or very poor condition for the selected category. Click a road to see its street, borough, PCI score, pavement condition and truck regulation.

Scope and limitations

This is a spatial comparison of pavement condition and truck regulations, not a claim that truck traffic causes pavement damage. Historical pavement change from 2020 to 2024 is not included in this iteration and could be a future extension.

Where this comes from

Pavement: Indicateurs de condition des chaussées du réseau routier — Ville de Montréal, CC BY 4.0.

Truck regulations: Règlements relatifs à la circulation des véhicules lourds et des véhicules outils sur le territoire de l'agglomération de Montréal — Ville de Montréal, CC BY 4.0.
