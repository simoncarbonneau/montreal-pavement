Montréal Arterial Pavement Condition — 2024 Snapshot

This map shows the 2024 pavement condition of Montréal's arterial road segments. It is the first part of a larger project comparing pavement condition with Montréal's truck-route network.

This map is for

Montréal borough infrastructure planners and road-maintenance teams.

What you can do here

Use the condition filters to show or hide pavement-condition classes, then click a road segment to see its street name, borough, PCI score and condition.

Not in this iteration

The truck-route comparison is not included in this version because it requires a second dataset and a more complex join. For A2, I focused on getting the pavement-condition map working properly first.

Where this comes from

Indicateurs de condition des chaussées du réseau routier, Ville de Montréal, CC BY 4.0. 2024 arterial network (RAAV). Accessed September 30, 2026.
